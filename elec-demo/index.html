COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Clear, straightforward answers about our services, response times, and engineering approach.

Do you provide same-day electrical service?
Yes. We provide same-day response for urgent electrical breakdowns, short circuits, and safety hazards depending on technician availability and location.
Do you provide complete house wiring?
Yes. We manage turnkey residential wiring projects, including concealed conduit laying, circuit design, load balancing, distribution board setup, and testing for new and renovated homes.
Do you work with commercial properties?
Yes. We provide complete commercial electrical fit-outs for offices, retail stores, healthcare clinics, and educational facilities, including three-phase panels and UPS backup circuits.
Do you provide warehouse electrical services?
Yes. We install high-bay industrial lighting, overhead cable trays, 3-phase machinery distribution panels, and heavy-duty grounding systems in warehouses and factories.
Do you provide CCTV installation?
Yes. We install high-definition IP cameras, network video recorders (NVRs), structured Cat6 cabling, and mobile app integration for residential and commercial security.
Do you provide earthing and lightning protection?
Yes. We design and install chemical and copper plate earth pits, verify ground resistance with calibrated earth testers, and install lightning arresters to safeguard buildings.
Do you offer annual maintenance contracts?
Yes. We offer customized Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for commercial buildings, residential societies, and industrial units with routine inspections and priority emergency support.
Can I send photos of an electrical problem?
Yes. You can send photos or short videos of your breaker panel, switchboard, or damaged electrical fixture directly to our WhatsApp number for preliminary assessment.
Do you provide quotations before planned work?
Yes. For planned installations, rewiring, and fit-outs, we conduct an inspection and provide an itemized, transparent quotation before commencing any work.
